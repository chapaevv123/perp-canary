# Wallet architecture audit — M2.2 (26 October 2026)

## Confirmed model and scope

The connected MetaMask/Rabby EOA is a login and possible funding/owner address, not automatically the account that owns dreamDEX orders. The app provisions a Privy-backed smart trading wallet; its signing key is an embedded key that the app does not expose. Official security guidance describes the model as non-custodial while noting that the login controls the account and the external wallet may be added as a second smart-account owner. The address of a user's app wallet is not derivable from the EOA through the public Markets SDK. Sources: [dreamDEX deposit/login guide](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/welcome/making-your-first-deposit), [dreamDEX account/key risks](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/security/risks).

Perps are on Hideki `50383`; the official developer guide states that the app and published SDK use `@somnia-chain/markets-sdk@0.32.1`. This confirms protocol/API alignment, not identical signing or transaction routing. A direct SDK `placePerpOrder` uses the configured signer; the app can use its embedded smart-wallet signer and account abstraction. A linked bot has its **own** key and gas, calls `placeOrderFor` for the owner wallet, and cannot withdraw. The bot's operator grants are per market/selector. Sources: [developer guide](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/developers/perps-testnet), [Link a Bot](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/common/link-a-bot), [operator permissions](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/spot/operators).

The wallet's spot USDso balance and its perps margin differ. Perps collateral is USDso held for the **trading account** in the MarginBank; `depositMargin`/`withdrawMargin` move it. Cross margin backs all positions of that account. Perps Arena's paper USDso is a restricted credit voucher counted in that same perps account balance; it is not an independent off-chain perp ledger or transferable wallet balance. The arena also grants testnet STT to each registered trading wallet. Sources: [Perps overview](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/perps-testnet), [margin and voucher rules](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/docs/trading/perps-testnet/margin-and-liquidations), [Arena rules](https://app.testnet.dreamdex.io/competition), [SDK perps](https://prd.smk.somnia.host/docs/typescript/perps).

The SDK also documents a **linked-wallet funding rail** for isolated perps accounts. That main/child funding relationship is separate from app login ownership and from Link a Bot operator authority. It needs two wallet signatures to link; a child sends its own position-increasing order, pays its own native gas, and may draw approved USDso from a main wallet. No such link is inferred for any Perp Canary user without a chain read. Source: [SDK linked wallets](https://prd.smk.somnia.host/docs/typescript/perps).

## What can be read without keys

Given a **public trading-account address**, SDK `getMarginAccount`, `getPerpPosition`, `getPerpPortfolio`, `listPerpOrderHistory`, and `getOrderOnchain` can read margin, position, indexed open/finished orders and individual on-chain orders. Hideki RPC can read USDso `balanceOf`, native STT balance, transaction and receipt. None requires a signer. The SDK does not expose a public authenticated lookup from a connected EOA to that user's dreamDEX login wallets. Source: [SDK perps](https://prd.smk.somnia.host/docs/typescript/perps); installed SDK 0.32.1 declarations and ABIs.

For a direct pool transaction, an existing plan and tx hash can enter M1 Expected vs Actual. An app transaction routed through a smart account or bot may have a top-level target and sender different from the pool and owner. The new diagnostic decodes top-level `placeOrder`/`cancelOrder` where possible and reads pool `OrderPlaced` owner logs. It marks a contract-mediated receipt as `AMBER` if pool or MarginBank activity exists but the inner call and signer are not proven. Full Expected vs Actual requires a plan saved **before** the transaction and proof of the inner operation; a historical hash alone cannot establish expected parameters or an earlier baseline. A real app tx hash was not provided for this audit, so this route remains unverified live.

## UNKNOWN and owner input

For the owner's account, the dreamDEX trading address, the EOA-to-app-wallet relationship, embedded signer, exact gas payer of a specific order, voucher amount and bot grants remain UNKNOWN until their public addresses or an existing tx hash are supplied and checked. A zero STT/USDso/MarginBank read on the external EOA is only a fact about that EOA. The diagnostic never combines it with another wallet or reports the missing trading wallet as zero.

Copy the public wallet address from dreamDEX's wallet list, choose the same Hideki pool in Perp Canary, and run Wallet Architecture Diagnostics. An existing app tx hash can then reveal the outer sender/target and pool owner events. Do not provide a seed, private key or new transaction.
