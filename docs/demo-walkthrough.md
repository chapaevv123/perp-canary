# Demo walkthrough

1. Run `npm.cmd run serve` and open `http://127.0.0.1:8787`. Select BTC-PERP, then another market from the dynamically discovered SDK/factory list. Show the selected pool, live mark, pinned book, on-chain grid and block. If the source is unavailable or stale, show the explicit UNKNOWN state.
2. Show Canary Market Radar. Spread, visible top-five depth share and mark movement come from trusted snapshots; watch alerts are in-tab only. Enter capital, risk percentage, limit entry and stop reference to demonstrate Smart Position Sizing. The stop is not installed, and the calculation excludes fees, funding, slippage and liquidation.
3. Connect the operator EIP-1193 wallet on Hideki 50383. Select **Direct Wallet** and show its own 5 STT and zero free margin. Select **Delegated Perps** and show Wallet 1 as owner with its separate free margin, position, open orders and per-pool place/cancel grants. No account balance is combined.
4. Enter a small, noncrossing BTC BUY PostOnly limit for Wallet 1 (at least 0.00013 BTC). A 10 bps offset below the current bid can reduce crossing risk; a fresh Guard may still block if the book moves. Review owner, operator, exact pool, margin preview, gas, nonce, expiry and calldata. Stop at wallet confirmation unless the owner explicitly chooses to send this particular testnet transaction.
5. Show the separate exact-amount approval and margin actions in Direct Wallet mode. Delegated Perps only offers owner-scoped cancellation for a currently open Wallet 1 order. Each action has its own simulation, review and wallet confirmation. The local journal distinguishes a submitted hash, receipt confirmation and later open-order observation. An absent order alone does not prove a fill. Show the independently verified filled order `14978756187852164141712` through Wallet 1 indexed history and the [read-only evidence bundle](evidence/verified-delegated-btc-order.json); this filled order is no longer cancellable.
6. Show the verified historical owner-bot BTC order in Owner diagnostics: successful receipt in block 34465778, `OrderPlaced`, `OrderRested`, order ID `14978756187852164026332`, and proof hash. The original short scan had cause `UNKNOWN`; a later full live scan can show `OrderExpired` when its matching event is read.
7. Close with limitations: one delegated user-wallet placement and fill is verified; no live cancellation, guaranteed reduce-only close, direct EOA trade, market/IOC order controls, complete historical PnL, background alert delivery or model-backed AI Copilot is verified. This is a local testnet demonstration, not a published trading service.

Owner-approved transaction checkpoint: **READY_FOR_OWNER_CONFIRMATION** after a real user wallet has funds and a fresh guarded review passes. Each state-changing transaction has its own wallet confirmation. No transaction is performed by this walkthrough.
